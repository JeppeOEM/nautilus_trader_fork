- (decision by user) MoSCoW: browser-handoff keybinding is deep-linked (exact coin + time-window/zoom), merged into Should tier - not a separate Could-tier enhancement

- (insight) Synthesis: shared ranking source of truth makes the coin-list 'mirror' actually true over time (not two drifting implementations); live-only-TUI + deep-linked browser handoff are one idea (cutting history only works because one keypress restores exact context on the website); scope-reversal-to-controls + color-discipline share one design principle - minimum surface area, maximum leverage, rare deliberate signals over either seeing or acting.
