Region 1 (snapshot) is always live from bots:status. Regions 2-3 read live_paper's own trade/PnL history surface (built over Nautilus's Cache, not a bespoke store — EXPERIENCE.md Data Sources & Staleness). Sparkline bars use only the DESIGN.md-specified block-element glyph set, colored by sign per bar. Flow 2's narrative beat: builder cycles day→week with t to find when the drop happened (bar 6 turns negative, wed), scrolls the blotter to the matching fills, confirms it's a bad market move rather than a bug, then hits o for the dashboard's fuller view and s to stop the bot for the day.
